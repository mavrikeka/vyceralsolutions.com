Evidence-Based Transformation Planning Framework

Four interconnected phases that build your AI and robotics roadmap from landscape to funded plan

Technology Landscape Assessment

Map every relevant AI and robotics capability on the hype cycle—so you know what's real, what's hype, and what's coming

What You Get

30-40 Capabilities Mapped

Every AI/robotics capability relevant to your value chain

Evidence-Graded

A-D grades based on production deployments, not vendor promises

Hype Cycle Positioning

Where each capability sits: Innovation → Mainstream adoption

How It Works

  1. Scan your entire value chain: field service, facilities, parts/warehouse, sales, back office
  2. Research 30-40 AI and robotics capabilities from published sources and expert interviews
  3. Place each on the hype cycle with evidence grade (A = proven results, D = vendor claims only)
  4. Mark time to mainstream adoption: <2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, 10+ years
  5. Identify who delivers it: OEM, Microsoft/cloud providers, third-party market, or you
  6. Assess adjoining industries (agriculture, aviation, HVAC) for early signals

Deliverables

  • Hype Cycle Chart: Visual map of all capabilities with maturity positioning
  • Capability Profiles: 12 in-depth profiles for priority capabilities
  • Evidence Sources: Named companies running each capability in production
  • Signals to Watch: 10-15 indicators that would move a capability earlier/later
  • First Priority Matrix: High/medium/low value estimates for board discussion

Value-at-Stake Modeling

Quantify the P&L impact of AI and robotics on your business—both upside opportunity and downside threat

What You Get

P&L Impact by Business Line

Dollars at stake, not vague percentages

Threat Register

Where disintermediation or margin compression could hurt

Workforce Scenarios

Technician supply/demand through 2031

How It Works

  1. Map Phase 1 capabilities onto your P&L by business line (parts, service, rental, etc.)
  2. Interview 8-12 senior leaders across operations, finance, and customer-facing roles
  3. Model workforce implications: technician supply, demand, where AI changes the constraint
  4. Analyze disintermediation risk: where OEM direct channels or customer-facing AI bypass you
  5. Identify 3-5 new revenue pools (e.g., deploying/servicing autonomous equipment for customers)
  6. Build 2-3 scenarios for your industry in 2031 (OEM-led, dealer-led, contested)

Deliverables

  • Value-at-Stake Model: Upside and threat ranges by business line with all formulas shown
  • Priority Matrix in Dollars: Phase 1 matrix updated with quantified value
  • Industry Scenarios: 2-3 futures for your sector with early warning signs
  • Threat Register: Where your role, margin, or customer relationship is at risk
  • Workforce View: What changes for technicians, parts, and sales roles

Investment Portfolio & Decision Framework

Make the calls: For each priority capability, do you lead, acquire, partner, or wait?

What You Get

Clear Decisions

Lead/Acquire/Partner/Wait for every priority

First-Mover Assessments

When to bet early vs. wait for proof

Acquisition Candidates

Screened companies to buy or invest in

The Decision Framework

  • Lead (Proven): Invest now in capabilities proven elsewhere (evidence grade A/B)
  • Lead (First-Mover): Bleeding-edge bets before results exist—with upside case, cost of being wrong, and exit plan
  • Acquire: Buy or invest in a company to own the capability outright
  • Partner: Co-invest or co-develop with OEM, vendor, or peer
  • Wait: Watch with a specific trigger that would change the call

No-Regret Moves: Data, skills, and process foundations you need regardless of which way the calls go

Deliverables

  • Investment Portfolio: Every priority with one of the five calls and rationale
  • Acquisition Screen: First-pass candidates with scale, stage, indicative value, and fit
  • No-Regret Moves: Foundations needed whichever way the calls go
  • OEM Influence Agenda: What you need from your OEM partner to protect your role and data
  • Leadership Workshop: Facilitated session to finalize the calls

Funded Roadmap & Business Cases

Turn decisions into action: Stage-gated business cases, investment envelopes, and board presentations

What You Get

Business Cases

3-5 Lead bets with stage gates and kill criteria

Investment Envelopes

2027-2029 funding: committed, gated, and option money

Board Presentation

Executive-ready slides with recommendations

How It Works

  1. Build business cases for 3-5 Lead bets at ±30% accuracy
  2. Define stage gates: what you learn at each step, when to kill, when to scale
  3. Design first-step pilots that prove the concept with minimal investment
  4. For Acquire calls: develop investment theses with target shortlists and commercial logic
  5. Structure investment envelope: committed funds, gated releases, option money
  6. Map operating model, workforce, and governance changes needed to execute
  7. Create refresh process: how to keep hype cycle and matrix current

Deliverables

  • Business Cases: 3-5 Lead bets with ROI, stage gates, kill criteria, first-step design
  • Investment Theses: Acquire targets with shortlist, value, and deal rationale
  • Investment Envelope: 2027-2029 funding plan with 5-year view
  • Operating Model: Workforce, governance, and process changes needed
  • Refresh Process: Twice-yearly review cadence to keep decisions current
  • Board Presentation: Executive slides ready for approval
