Failure

Created on 2016-06-13 11:36

Published on 2016-06-13 12:08

I would never have thought I'd write a post about failure. I have always prided myself on being a success. I was a good student, good at sports, good at work but lately I've been realizing that I needed to acknowledge the importance of failure in my success.

1. Failure is life's best teacher, but you need a second chance and there is always a second chance.

2. Trying something new and failing will teach you more about yourself, than succeeding at something you can do in your sleep.

3. It is better to not have the answers, but to instead have a lot of hypotheses to test.

4. Learn by doing is another way of saying, try-fail-try-fail...try-fail-succeed

5. It isn't failure that you should worry about, it is giving up.

6. Everyone talks about 'fail fast', but you should really be able to 'recover fast'.

7. The success of past failures often manifests itself much later.

In summary, two words that my teachers would never have used together - failure and learning have turned out to be two sides of the same coin. I've realized that everything I've learnt has been the result of failing at something.

So, don't call it Failing call it Learning.

and lastly

Sometimes success is knowing when to give up, move on or giving it time.
