COVID-19 : Are we out of the woods?

Created on 2020-03-27 19:38

Published on 2020-03-27 20:50

Not sure if anyone else is in the same boat as I am, but the last couple days have felt less doom and gloom to me than before. Maybe it is because Congress is taking action on the stimulus bill, maybe it is because our CEO Marc Benioff has pledged no layoffs for 90 days, maybe it is because the stock market has stopped hemorrhaging, maybe I'm slowly getting used to the new normal, maybe it is because spring is here, the flowers are blooming and the birds are chirping - but overall it has felt like there is light at the end of the tunnel.

So I decided to do some data driven analysis to see if the way I was feeling could be proven with data. I asked myself the question - is the rate of growth of cases slowing down? are we seeing fewer cases daily? how did the rate of cases look for China.

Sadly, the data did not support my optimism. You can check out this visualization with my simple analysis - https://public.tableau.com/profile/vik1873#!/vizhome/COVID-19-DailyCases/COVID-19Areweoutofthewoods

As you can see above, the number of new cases in the US is not just going up but just jumped yesterday (03/26). This can be because more states are testing but it still is not a good data point. This is how China's cases look (even with a big outlier on Feb 15th there is definitely a lift and then a drop.

Trying to find a silver lining I went looking for state level data. Is there good news to be found in states like Washington, California and New York that were affected early on. Are they slowing down? In the chart below, each block is a state and the number of cases are on the Y axis and the date on the X axis.

Of all the states Washington was seeing a decline but then has had a spike recently. So there is some hope there but we have to see how the next couple days look. Not only are the cases in NY and CA going up but we are also starting to see a spike in cases in other states (FL, PA, IL to name a few). The graph I was hoping to see was something like below. You can see the bell curves that show the spike and then cases falling.

Just like it is hard to call the bottom on this stock market, it is hard to say if we are at the apex of the crisis or still got some time to go. I guess for me it means staying vigilant. I cannot let my guard down. Keep the quarantine going, wash hands and keep working from home.
