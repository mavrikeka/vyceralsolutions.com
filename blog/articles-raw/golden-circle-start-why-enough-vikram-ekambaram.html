Golden Circle - 'Start with the Why'​ is not enough

Created on 2019-08-10 11:57

Published on 2019-08-12 12:01

I am a big fan of Simon Sinek's Golden Circle. I still remember the first time I saw his Ted Talk and it just made complete sense. I've embraced 'Start with Why' in a lot of what I do. It has really helped me be ask more questions, approach things with a Beginner's mind and just be curious. I really felt that he had figured out a way to 'inspire action' and for me to be a good leader and at a gut level, it felt right.

Lately, I've realized that while 'Why, How, What' helps with setting high level vision it has gaps when it comes down to execution. It was not necessarily inspiring the 'right' action. I manage a Customer Success team and while we've been good about explaining the 'Why, How, What' it has definitely led to gaps in execution, gaps in people being able to quantify their impact, gaps in us communicating impact to the broader company, gaps in prioritization. The realization that I've come to is the importance of measuring impact in addition to the 'Why, How, What.'. So what seems to make more sense is - "Why, Measure Impact, How, What".

I will caveat the rest of this piece with the comment that Simon Sinek is super famous, a gazillionaire (I am sure), has done a lot lot more research etc. and this is not in any way meant to be a diss against his work. This is just my experience in applying the Golden Circle at work and the tweak I have felt needed to be done for my situation. I will also caveat by saying that I could word our why, how, what much better. I'll take our example as a Customer Success organization to explain the need for the tweak.

Why - "At Tableau we have a relentless customer focus in helping customers see and understand data thus creating customers for life"

How - "Customer Success helps customers maximize the value of the investment they have made in Tableau"

What - "We understand customers key strategic priorities, assess their current state, identify a prioritized list of actions that we document in a Success Plan and execute on. We maintain a regular engagement cadence with customers, help them build community, be the bridge with our product teams and document the value customers get from Tableau".

As I mentioned earlier, the wording above can be tweaked and maybe a Golden Circle purist will say we've not got it completely right but I am hoping I can explain the challenges we have faced, with the above example.

So, while a Customer Success Manager knows the above, some challenges we have faced are -

  1. We still get questions from our Sales counterparts (other teams within Tableau) - "What do you really do" or "I don't get it" or "This seems like a lot of words" etc.
  2. Our Customer Success Managers (CSM) are working really really hard. For those who don't know, a CSM is part Consultant, part Project Manager, part Technical expert, part Marketing Event coordinator, part Therapist etc. There are tons of things to do. End of the day we are on the hook to make the customer successful. While the "Why, How, What" gives us direction it does not help us prioritize. It is hard to know what is critical, what is a nice to do and we end up with "Blind Spots".
  3. Customers sometimes find it hard to engage as they are all at different parts of their journey and they are all unique. So it is hard for them to really understand the impact of what we do and thus see value in engaging.
  4. Sometimes we become tactical and become too focused on executing a to-do list and are not able to step back and be strategic
  5. Drive the change management needed

So this has led to us tweaking the "Why, How, What" by adding in "Measuring Impact". We've borrowed the 'North Star Metric' concept. We also incorporated 'Leading and Lagging indicators' or as our head of sales likes to ask us - "Are you weighing the pig or feeding the pig". I'll write a separate post about North Star Metric and Leading / Lagging indicators. So this is how our tweaked 'Golden Circle' looks like -

Why - "At Tableau we have a relentless customer focus in helping customers see and understand data thus creating customers for life"

Measure Impact - We know we are creating customers for life when the Net Retention Rate (NRR) for a customer is going up (lagging indicator) and we impact the NRR by Engaging with the customer, Getting them to Adopt and Utilize the product they bought, Documenting the Value via use cases, Monitoring the Health of the customer and identifying opportunities for Expanding the relationship with the customer (leading indicators).

How - "Customer Success helps customers maximize the value of the investment they have made in Tableau"

What - "We understand customers key strategic priorities, assess their current state, identify a prioritized list of actions that we document in a Success Plan and execute on. We maintain a regular engagement cadence with customers, help them build community, be the bridge with our product teams and document the value customers get from Tableau".

Another caveat, I am not attached to whether the order is Why, MI (Measure Impact), How, What or Why, How, MI, What. The point is that adding MI allows us to communicate impact to key stakeholders, allows CSMs to prioritize, identify Blind Spots and risks, be intentional about the Change Management that this entails etc.

Bottomline, adding MI has allowed us to inspire the 'right' action (I am sure there are folks that will argue about whether we are really inspiring but that is a different discussion). Curious if I am the only one facing this?
