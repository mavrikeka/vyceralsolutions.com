Automating Account Planning

Created on 2024-02-01 12:29

Published on 2024-02-01 13:29

In my previous edition of this newsletter, I wrote about how GenAI could save hours of time for an AE or CSM that is doing account research. That being said, I still saw inefficiencies in the process. It still takes about 5 hrs for an AE to do the research and then present the POV to the customer.

As I was thinking through this process and putting on my process optimization hat, the product idea I had in my head was, what if the AE did not have to do any of the work of going to ChatGPT, putting in the PDF, ask questions etc. Wouldn't it be amazing, if an AE could open up his CRM and the information was already populated for his customer in his CRM. Eliminate the account research part of the process and return another hour to the AEs.

As I was chatting with Simon Frey, he pointed me to Copy.ai and said I should check it out. Part of it was because Simon and I both geek out about outcomes and the tag line for Copy.ai was "The only AI platform purpose-built for outcomes". As I am evaluating options for my next job, he felt that I should check out Copy.ai as a company I could work for as he shares my passion for Outcomes.

As I started doing research on Copy.ai, I noticed that it was the product that I had been thinking about. It is a GenAI wrapper (I am sure I am not doing them justice) that allows you to build a workflow to automate tasks. So I started playing around with it and I did a quick test but it was exactly what I was visualizing. You describe the workflow and it generates the steps and does all the work.

I just gave Copy.ai my instructions in plain english and it generated this 4 step workflow. Jill Markoff Devon Kirschmann Stephanie Pellegrino , you know how much I love my flow diagrams. The first step generates a form where I share the link to the 10K and the name of my company, then it scrapes the data from the PDF (I could have given it a URL or something else), then it calls GenAI with my prompts (you can edit and reconfigure all of this) and then outputs to PDF, but I could call a Zapier API to update CRM or integrate with any other system. Check out this video.

To expand on this further, you could also upload your value proposition training, your CVI Why Change messaging, any product collateral and all that can be used as input to generate very tailored and specific point of views for your customers.

The ROI for this is very obvious - Let's say you have 100 accounts you want to do Account Plans for.

  • Current Process No GenAI - 850 hours

  • New Process with AE using GenAI - 550 hours

  • New Process with Copy.ai - 250 hours + 1 time set up + license cost.

To be fair, I just played around with Copy.ai and I am not sure what is involved in getting this all working for a production use case but I can see the vision of where they are going with automating workflows and I can see how GenAI will start delivering specific business use cases that people in the field can see the immediate impact in time savings. I can see how I can quantify the 'Outcomes'.
