Brain Atrophy

Created on 2015-11-12 23:11

Published on 2015-11-13 00:35

Lately I have been pretty active on the social media scene (I guess that is an understatement). Friends who have known me have been asking what is going on? More importantly, how are you coming up with all these ideas to post on.

To answer this I am imagining the term 'Brain Atrophy'. Not sure if it is an official term to describe what was going on, but it really resonates with me. I feel that when it came to creativity and curiosity, I had let my brain atrophy. What has been eye opening for me is that once I tapped into those aspects, I really turned the spigot on.

I took the first hesitant step and posted something, then something else. Before you know it, I started looking at things differently. Take a random saying  - "Curiosity killed the cat". In the past I would have gone on, but now I stop and ask myself - that does not make sense and before you know it, there is the beginning of an idea for a post. Then it is like a piece of creative work where I play around with some ideas in my head and come up with a story.  I also noticed that by putting myself out there, people comment about a post, give some positive feedback or ask a question which then leads to another post. I have started keeping a list of topics that I update as and when inspiration hits me (when I wake up, before going to sleep, driving etc.). Suddenly, I now see topics all around me.

This also reminds me of a comment some of my customers have made when they use Tableau. They say that it has made them more analytical and curious. Tools they used in the past, were clunky and took too much time to analyze data or required a high level of expertise. So they stopped being curious. They answered the same questions month after month and slowly their analytical capacities atrophied. Any time someone asked a new question, it was 'work' and not fun. Then they discovered Tableau and they were changed. The analyst within them found a voice and they were off to the races. Suddenly they were analyzing and answering all kinds of questions that in the past they would not have considered answering.  Ken Black a Tableau user from GM tweeted a couple days ago - "A man of your intellect needs to have that tool. It will unleash your inner beast."

In case you are wondering why I let my brain atrophy. Here is what my brain was busy telling me - I am not analytical, I am not curious, I am not creative, I don't have the time, I am too busy, people will judge my posts / analysis, this seems like too much work, I am not interesting, I'll run out of ideas etc.Luckily I gave it some exercise and unleashed my inner beast.
