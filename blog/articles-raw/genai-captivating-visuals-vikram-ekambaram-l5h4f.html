I have been waiting a long time for this and I recently discovered Napkin.ai. I assume this has been available for sometime now but my experience in the past working with GenAI image generator tools was not that great.

Some background first. I suck at creating captivating visuals for my slides. My content is always rich but my visual design skills leave a lot to be desired. If this were the SAT, I would get 800 on content and 500 on design.

I have always been in awe of people like Jeremy Blaney, Brandon Ralls, Katie Summers , Jonathan Dugger who could spin up stunning visuals. I was also extremely aware of how good visuals can make such a big difference in any presentation but try as I might I could never get the colors right, I could not get the font right, I did not know how to present the information with the right visual and whatever I created was blah.

What hurt even more was that I spent 8 years at Tableau surrounded by amazing visual thinkers.

An artificial intelligence (AI) agent is a software program that can interact with its environment, collect data, and use the data to perform self-determined tasks to meet predetermined goals. Humans set goals, but an AI agent independently chooses the best actions it needs to perform to achieve those goals. For example, consider a contact center AI agent that wants to resolves customer queries. The agent will automatically ask the customer different questions, look up information in internal documents, and respond with a solution. Based on the customer responses, it determines if it can resolve the query itself or pass it on to a human.
