Can GenAI write a blog post

Created on 2024-02-07 18:37

Published on 2024-02-07 18:56

I had posted yesterday my experience in leveraging ChatGPT in the process of setting up an LLC. The meta experiment I wanted to try out today was whether I could have ChatGPT write an article about my experience. I hear all the time about how people use ChatGPT to generate blogs and I just could not comprehend how it could do that. In the comments below I will post the link to the article I wrote and the article ChatGPT wrote and I will let you be the judge.

My 0.02 - While ChatGPT could definitely generate a good enough post about my experience, in the amount of time it would take me to generate the correct prompt, iterate on it to get the article just right, I could write the article.

If you want ChatGPT to generate something based on stuff that is already out there (information) then it will do great. If you want ChatGPT to generate something based on an experience you have had, it can do it but it is a lot of work to come up with the prompt.
