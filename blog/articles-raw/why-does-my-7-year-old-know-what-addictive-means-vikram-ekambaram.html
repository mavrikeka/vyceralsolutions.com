Why does my 7 year old know what 'addictive' means?

Created on 2015-11-06 16:10

Published on 2015-11-06 16:35

When I was growing up, I am pretty sure that at the age of 7 I did not know what the word 'addiction' meant and I am 100% sure I never used it to describe anything I did or had experienced 'addiction'. I liked reading, I liked playing with my friends, I loved sports but I was never addicted to anything. My 7 year old has been playing Clash of Clans for the last couple months and he will often ask permission to get on the iPad to collect coins / elixirs etc. He got me playing the game a few weeks ago. A few days ago he announced that he was 'addicted' to it. It was a funny statement and since I have also been playing Clash of Clans I can totally relate to him.

There is some good news and bad news - The good news is that we have one more thing to bond and connect around. We can have conversations around strategies, how to defend better, who to attack etc. There is also, definitely an element of learning and teaching. We can discuss how someone attacked us and breached our defenses and what we need to do to fix it. It has also been one way to have him get up on time in the morning and catch the school bus (you got 5 mins to check how your clan is doing, if you get ready early).

The bad news is that I can totally relate to the addictive nature of these apps. The sad news is that the bar, on being entertained, is being raised so high. My realization as a parent is that limits become critical. Teaching our kids balance becomes critical too. Every generation has it's version (it was TV shows back in the day) and as parents it is easy to criticize our kids rather than relate to them. There is a lot of temptation out there.

On that note - I need to go.. Time to check how my clan is doing and plot the next attack.
