Sales Compensation Plans - There has got to be a better way

Created on 2015-11-06 17:08

Published on 2015-11-06 17:51

I am sitting on a flight and just finished reading a post from Reid Hoffman around power of purpose at work. I also heard Daniel Pink talk a couple weeks ago about how Autonomy, Purpose, Mastery are key to motivating employees. I've been lucky to get a job where I do feel this sense of purpose and it is motivating. I wake up energized (it's been 2 years, so that is a record for me). I totally dig what these guys are saying. I can relate to it.

It got me thinking about my past sales jobs at large and fairly respectable companies. I remember joining those companies and I had a great sense of purpose. I believed in what they stood for. Their mission, their beliefs. But the shine disappeared after the initial honeymoon period.

Lately, I've been thinking about what caused it and I realized that the way sales people are compensated is totally broken. You make your number you feel motivated you miss your number you feel like a failure. It didn't matter if the company was great, it didn't matter if your team did great, you felt like a loser if you didn't make your number. I spent 6 years in those companies not making my number and I know how it feels first hand to not make my number. You doubt yourself, your confidence disappears and unless you are someone that is very strong internally it can totally deflate you.

How did I handle those 6 years - I introspected, I talked to my peers, I talked to my managers, I talked to my customers. Was it something I was doing or was it systemic? I fixed things in the areas I controlled and I found external ways to feel good about myself - picked up tennis, learnt about the theory of relativity, solved a rubik's cube etc. I kept my lifestyle simple and took money out of the equation. But the jobs sucked.

I remember one manager giving me a bad territory and a big quota. He told me that I was the new guy on the team and I had to pay my dues. How did I know that it was a bad territory and a big quota? One of the reps had moved to a new role and I got his territory and quota, but they took all the good accounts and gave it to the other more 'tenured' reps but they kept the quota. One of the companies I worked for had a 75% line. If you didn't make 75% of your number you made no commission. What was worse is that if you were at 75% halfway through the year, they paid you your commission but if at the end of the year you were below 75% you had to give it back (and deal with the tax implications). Needless to say I didn't stick around too long in that company.

The last couple years I've made my number. I feel like a rock star. But I am the same person. Which brings me to the topic of this post - Sales compensation is broken. We all know that success in sales is about - Territory, Timing and Talent. Guess what - you don't control 2 of the 3. We all know that while there are a few exceptional sales people, most successful sales people got help from the right Territory and Timing. How many of us know salespeople that had all the Talent but did not make their number? How many of us know salespeople that made their number one year but missed the next. Are they not good anymore? Why do we have a compensation system that treats them like they've failed? To make it worse, not just is it a hit on a person's self belief and confidence, there is a direct hit on your paycheck.

Large companies are starting to do away with performance reviews, they are experimenting with new ways to motivate people, they are doing away with fixed PTO days, sales managers have moved from being tyrannical to collaborative. Companies realize that people are their most important asset. If people are your most important asset why have a system in place that will de-motivate a chunk of your sales force? If you believe that you hire the 'right' people, then why have a system that sets them up for failure (sometimes every quarter).

If we know that money is an extrinsic motivator and intrinsic motivators like Autonomy, Purpose, Mastery are more important shouldn't there be a better way? Is someone ready to disrupt this?
