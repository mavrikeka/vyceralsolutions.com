Binge-watching, Boredom and Time

Created on 2015-12-31 13:44

Published on 2015-12-31 14:10

One of my favorite Pink Floyd songs is Time. As we get to the end of 2016 and I wonder where the time went and how another year went by so quickly I am reminded of some lyrics from Time -

"So you run and you run to catch up with the sun but it's sinking
Racing around to come up behind you again. "

"Every year is getting shorter; never seem to find the time."

We were at a tennis camp and I realized that part of the reason 'time' is going by so fast is because of this 'always on' world we live in. Even on a vacation trip I am booked pretty full. Add to that checking emails, catching up on stuff on digital devices and there really isn't much down-time.

This digital world we live in feeds into our instant gratification needs. Any moment of down time is filled up by reading news on a device, playing a game on your phone, connecting with someone, checking Facebook etc. We're also creating a world where kids aren't used to being bored. We fill their schedules with after school activities, play dates etc. Give them a few minutes where they have nothing and they want to go on a digital device. They don't know what to do if nothing is going on. The trend towards consuming media on devices celebrates binge-watching. Back in the day you watched a show and you were done, now you watch 3,5 or more in a row. My sons want to know what is wrong with watching multiple shows in a row. The content is so engaging that I can see how it is hard to turn off the show. I binge watch myself, so who am I to tell them no.

Growing up in India with no digital distractions, I had time to create imaginary worlds in my head. A little boredom was cherished as a way to feed into my creativity. Sadly the genie is out of the bottle and the future we are creating will celebrate binge-watching over boredom.

I think about the lyrics of Time and I wonder if anyone will ever write this again

"Ticking away the moments that make up a dull day
Fritter and waste the hours in an offhand way. "

Maybe the new lyrics will be -

"Checking Facebook in between a busy day, binge-watch shows on Netflix every day"
