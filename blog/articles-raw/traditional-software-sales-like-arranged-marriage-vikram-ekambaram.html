Traditional Software Sales is like an Arranged Marriage

Created on 2015-10-16 02:15

Published on 2015-10-16 03:02

Imagine there are two families - A customer family and a software vendor family. The parents of the customer family are IT and Procurement and the kids the business units (let's say daughters). The parents of the vendor family is the Sales team and their kids the software that the vendors sell (let's say the sons).

It all starts with the parents of the customer family deciding that it is time to marry off one of their daughters. So they come up with a shortlist of vendor families and send off a request for proposal (RFP) for marriage. The vendor families then respond with a lot of detail around the traits of their sons. After reviewing the responses and getting the blessings of an astrologer (consultant), they shortlist down to a few vendor families.

Now the next stage of the selection kicks off. The Proof of Concept (POC) where the parents of the customer family spends time (multiple weeks) with each vendor family and discuss traits of the sons. Sometimes this is done with powerpoint and sometimes the vendors actually bring their sons out and have them do a 'show and tell'. At this 'show and tell', both kids get to meet and check each other out. After a parade of such suitors, it is time to make a decision. There is much negotiation and a dowry and gifts are decided upon and the marriage is finalized. The kids get married but sadly in today's world there is no happily ever after.

What the parents (IT) of the daughter don't realize is that their daughters (business units) aren't sheltered any more. Gone are the days when they were not allowed outside the house and they had no choice on who they married. They are quite independent and world wise. They have been meeting suitors from other families without their parents even knowing. These are new families that go by the name of Cloud and Mobile and Self Service.

These new families have a 'land and expand' strategy. They realize that to win the hand of these fair maidens they can't just strut their stuff and list out their features. This is a long term relationship and both parties want to be sure that this is the right decision. So they go out on dates, they get to know each other.  Before she invests a lot of time in the relationship she wants to make sure that her friends approve. So they meet her friends. Then they get to meet her cousins and siblings. At each step in the process the relationship gets solidified and finally the parents find out that their daughter wants to marry someone from another family. Imagine how the conversation goes when they tell their daughter that they want her to marry someone they have selected or that she should not be hasty and let them run a selection process to find the right groom!!
