Unleashing Human Potential

Created on 2015-12-03 13:11

Published on 2015-12-03 14:19

I binge watched 7 sessions last night from  TC15 (Tableau Conference). It does require a login. I was thinking, who binge watches conference sessions. Aren't they supposed to be boring? Aren't conferences supposed to be a boondoggle? Given a choice, why would someone watch conference sessions (let alone binge watch) rather than a show on Netflix. I even tweeted this fact proudly. I was questioning my ability to make good decisions in life :-)

What kept me going were the customer stories. Session after session was someone that was passionate, creative and excited. Hearing their stories (sometimes fact is better than fiction - paraphrasing Mark Twain) about the impact Tableau had on their careers, lives and companies was extremely addictive. It reminded me of why I wake up excited in the morning.

For me, working at Tableau has been a unique experience and the way it had impacted the lives of our customers, is the way I have felt about its impact on my life. Yesterday at 12:30 am when I finally got thrown out of the room by my wife for keeping her up, three words got crystallized - "Unleashing Human Potential". To me - that is what Tableau stands for. That is why we do what we do. That is what I heard in the customer stories. At Tableau, we have always believed that people are smart and creative. We empower individuals to unlock their ideas and contribute in ways they consider to be the highest use of their skills, intellect and curiosity. This came across loud and clear in the sessions.

I'll share one example from an Arby's presentation. They had been building reports and answering the same questions, until leadership told everyone - "Tell me something about Arby's that I don't already know!". For most business users, this would be terrifying. We all have questions and hypothesis that we are curious about. We can somehow wrangle the data. But how do I actually find those nuggets in the data that are the unknown unknowns? How do I tell my leadership something new about their business? At Arby's they had Tableau. Instead of scaring everyone, that thought actually spurred them on to "achieving an ongoing culture of curiosity and business analytics". Human potential had been unleashed.

This theme repeated itself in all the sessions - individuals that were empowered, were making a difference, were driving real change, were building analytical cultures in their organizations, were impacting the world, were proud of what they were achieving, were curious and were creative. I kept hearing - IT and business working together to drive business value rather than the typical strife between IT and Business.

From a more personal standpoint - last week was Thanksgiving and as I was thinking about what I am thankful for - I was reminded of how Tableau has helped me unleash my potential - whether it is my recent blogging or being perceived as family by my customers or finding my voice or being a leader or empowering others. I know a lot of it has to do with the environment I am in and the people I work with. As someone famous once said - "Long Live Data".
