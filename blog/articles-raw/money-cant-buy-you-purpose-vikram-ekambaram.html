Money can't buy you purpose

Created on 2015-10-15 12:17

Published on 2015-10-15 12:54

We've all heard the Beatles line - "money can't buy you love", but lately there has been another line going on in my head - "money can't buy you purpose". The Lamar Odom tragedy has been all over the news and I have no other information than what is in the news, but it somehow triggered that statement in my head.

Now this is pure conjecture but it looks like basketball gave him purpose but once that was over all he was left with was money. Coming from India and now living in the US, I've always wondered if people are happier when there is a struggle. When your basic needs are met, then you are looking for a higher purpose and that is very hard to find. The money is there but the purpose is missing.

I remember 2 jobs where I felt I was drifting. I was doing fine economically but felt there was something missing. I put all my energy into learning to play golf and I got real good. Then I hit a plateau and suddenly it seemed like work (probably partly due to the fact that I had started playing tennis). So I put in all my energy into learning how to play tennis. I found a community, found competition through USTA and I was driven. Now it's been about 7 years since I started playing tennis and I've transferred all that energy into my job. Tennis is fun but that 'high' is missing.

The questions I ask myself  - Can there be one purpose that lasts a lifetime or if you don't have that are you destined to keep shuttling from one fulfilling 'high' to the next? Why can't I enjoy the little things? Or is being in a new situation and the constant learning that comes from problem solving what drives me?
