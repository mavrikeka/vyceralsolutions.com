I was reading a post about leveraging GenAI for productivity from rubenhassid.ai where he talks about using ChatGPT with Simon Sinek's start with why.. and it got me thinking about using ChatGPT to create my new business's why. That being said, when it comes to building messaging I tend to leverage Corporate Visions 'Why Change, Why Now, Why Me' framework.

The Challenge with ChatGPT As much as I adore ChatGPT for its brilliance, there’s a catch - it's not quite there yet when it comes to highly targeted, specific content generation. When I used Generic ChatGPT the content was good but it took some liberty with the messaging and I realized that what I needed to do was feed it specific examples, so it could be more targeted. BUT HOW? How do I give it specific content?

Enter AnyQuest I stumbled upon AnyQuest, an orchestration layer that supercharges ChatGPT's capabilities. Special thanks to Dmitri Tcherevik and Helen Fu Thomas for giving me access to their platform. Imagine being able to feed ChatGPT with precise documents and getting back exactly what you need for your go-to-market strategy. Basically ChatGPTing specific content that you upload and that is secure 🤯!

Real-world Application I put this to the test with my own startup's strategy, uploading everything from market analyses to corporate vision documents. The result? A nuanced, targeted presentation that hits every note perfectly. AnyQuest has effectively bridged the gap, making ChatGPT an even more powerful tool for strategic planning.
