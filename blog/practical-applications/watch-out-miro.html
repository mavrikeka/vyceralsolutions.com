Watch out Miro!!

February 10, 2024 • 2 min read • GenAI in GTM

Click here if you want to watch a video instead of reading this.

My wife recently self published her first book and it was a huge labor of love. I could never do it and I am so proud of what she has accomplished. As most of you know it is hard for self published authors to stand out and we were discussing if she could do something on Instagram to create a following for her and the book.

Now I am not on Instagram and not a big marketer and so I was at a loss, but as I have been doing, I asked myself - WWGD? What Would GenAI Do?

This time instead of just having it give me text, I wanted to see if I could have it generate a MindMap. For those of you who know me, you know I love my Miro and LucidSpark and looking at these ideas visually. So I went down the rabbithole of - Can GenAI generate a MindMap of an instagram campaign.

I gave it the prompt and here is the output (there is a custom GPT called WhimsicalDiagrams) -

I am pretty impressed. It took me 5 mins to get this. Now I can go and edit it and make changes but it does make me ask myself, why would I create this from scratch? I am not saying that this is the final version but it is way easier for me to now edit this than create it from scratch.
