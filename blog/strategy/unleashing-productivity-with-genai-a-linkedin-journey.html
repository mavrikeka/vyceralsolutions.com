Unleashing Productivity with GenAI - A LinkedIn Journey

February 20, 2024 • 2 min read • GenAI in GTM

Hey, folks! 🌟

In my latest dive into the GenAI world, I've stumbled upon a goldmine of productivity hacks that I just had to share with you all. Imagine this: you've got a video full of insights and ideas that you're itching to share. What's the next step? For me, it's transforming that video into a comprehensive article and a snappy LinkedIn post to amplify its reach. But, let's be real, that process can be a bit... tedious.

Enter GenAI, my newfound savior! 🚀

In a recent experiment, I took a video I had made about outcomes mapping with GenAI and decided to put GenAI's capabilities to the test. Could it transform my video into a polished article and create a catchy LinkedIn summary post? Spoiler alert: it absolutely did.

I used a custom GPT tool designed specifically for video summarization. All it took was feeding the URL of my YouTube video into this magical tool, and voilà! It generated a detailed blog post that captured all the essential points from the video – the challenges, the process, and the outcomes. And it didn't stop there; it even crafted a summary post for LinkedIn, complete with hashtags and an image.

The beauty of this experiment wasn't just in the automation but in how the tool adapted its output in my voice, making the content feel personal and authentic. It's a game-changer for content creators who are looking to streamline their workflow without sacrificing the quality or personal touch of their content.

So, here's to more productivity hacks with GenAI that make our lives a tad easier and our content a whole lot richer! 🥂
